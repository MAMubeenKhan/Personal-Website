How I work

The real risk is someone disappearing at 60%

It is why most people hesitate, and nobody says it out loud. So here is how I take it off the table.

01

You describe it

A paragraph is enough. What it should do, who it is for. I will tell you honestly if I am the wrong person — that answer costs you a day instead of a month.

02

Fixed scope, fixed price

Within 48 hours you get written scope and a fixed number, not an hourly estimate that grows. If the scope changes later, we agree the change before it gets built.

03

A build you can run, weekly

Every week you get something installable, not a progress report. You see the real thing early enough to change your mind while changing it is still cheap.

About

Most software never gets published. Mine does.

I am Mohammed Abdul Mubeen Khan, a solo developer working from India.

The distance between a working prototype and something a stranger can download is where most projects quietly die. Privacy policies, data-safety forms, permission justifications, trader verification, rejections that arrive with no explanation. I have crossed that distance on Google Play, the Microsoft Store and the Chrome Web Store, and I know what each of them asks for before it asks.

I work fast, and AI tooling is a large part of how — but I read everything that ships, because the person who has to fix it in month six is me. Speed is not the pitch. Finishing is.
